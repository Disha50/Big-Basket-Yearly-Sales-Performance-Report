# 🛒 Big Basket Yearly Sales & Performance Dashboard

![Dashboard Preview](Big%20Basket%20yearly%20sale%20and%20report.png)

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Big Basket Yearly Sales & Performance Dashboard** is an interactive data visualization and business intelligence project designed to analyze sales performance, customer orders, revenue distribution, and operational trends.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of key business metrics across **months, states, customer demographics, sales channels, order statuses, and product categories**.

This project helps identify sales trends, high-performing regions, popular sales channels, and order-status patterns to support data-driven business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this dashboard are:

- Analyze monthly sales and order trends.
- Identify the **top-performing states by revenue**.
- Understand sales contribution across different channels.
- Analyze sales based on customer **age groups**.
- Compare sales contribution by **gender**.
- Examine the distribution of different **order statuses**.
- Provide interactive filtering for deeper analysis.
- Create a visually appealing and easy-to-understand business dashboard.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Highlights

### 1. Monthly Sales & Orders Trend

The dashboard displays monthly:

- **Total Sales Amount**
- **Number of Orders**

This helps identify seasonal patterns and changes in sales performance throughout the year.

### 2. Top 5 States by Revenue

The dashboard highlights the top revenue-generating states, including:

- Maharashtra
- Karnataka
- Uttar Pradesh
- Telangana
- Tamil Nadu

This provides insight into regional sales performance.

### 3. Sales by Age Group

Customers are segmented into different age groups to understand which demographic contributes the most revenue.

Example segments include:

- 18–24
- 25–34
- 35–44
- 45–54
- 55+

### 4. Sales Contribution by Gender

The dashboard compares sales contribution between:

- Men
- Women

This helps understand customer demographics and purchasing patterns.

### 5. Order Status Distribution

Orders are categorized based on their status:

- Cancelled
- Delivered
- Refunded
- Returned

This provides an overview of order fulfillment and potential operational issues.

### 6. Sales Contribution by Channel

Revenue is analyzed across multiple sales channels, such as:

- Amazon
- Flipkart
- Myntra
- Meesho
- Ajio
- Nalli
- Others

This helps identify which channels contribute the most to overall sales.

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes interactive slicers that allow users to dynamically filter the data.

Available filters include:

- **Channel**
- **Category**
- **Month**
- **Gender**
- **Status**

These filters make it possible to perform focused analysis without modifying the underlying dataset.

## 📌 Key Business Insights

From the dashboard, several useful observations can be made:

- Monthly sales show a changing trend throughout the year.
- **March** records one of the strongest sales and order performances.
- **Maharashtra** is the leading state among the top 5 states by revenue.
- **Karnataka** and **Uttar Pradesh** also contribute significantly to revenue.
- Customer sales can be analyzed effectively through age-group segmentation.
- Sales are distributed across multiple online channels, allowing channel-level performance comparison.
- The majority of orders are delivered, while cancelled, refunded, and returned orders represent smaller portions of the overall order volume.

> **Note:** Insights may change depending on the filters selected in the dashboard.
